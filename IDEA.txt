--- NUMBER GAMBLING ---
Numbers have more or less value depending on their qualities(between 1 - 999):
-if it has on number => SUPER rare
-if it has two number => Rare but not as much
-if its all the same number (33, 555, 111)
-if its divisable (by 2, 3, 4, 5, 10 ... will have to gauge)
-if its divisable by 100 (200, 400 ... again, will have to gauge)
-if its recognisable (21, 67, 69, 666, 777, 911)

All of the qualities will stack to give it a STAR rating ON TOP of the rarity
ie: Legendary 4 stars, common 5 stars, rare 1 stars
